A1.1 Kalibrasi skor relevansi lintas sumber (berlaku di mode Campur DAN Prioritas)

Laporanmu sendiri: rata-rata skor Openverse 0.920, Wikimedia 0.897, Pixabay 0.730. Di proyek lama Zodiac (mode Prioritas) Wikimedia juga memilih gambar tidak relevan: koran berbahasa Persia untuk "He called himself the Zodiac" dan peta satelit untuk "quiet suburbs of the San Francisco Bay Area". Skor sumber berbasis judul/deskripsi (Wikimedia, Openverse, NASA) terlalu tinggi karena kecocokan kata harfiah.
- Reproduksi dengan narasi uji di bawah. Tampilkan rincian skor per komponen (subject, query overlap, visual keyword, anchor, bonus narasi, penalti) untuk 3 kandidat teratas shot "apprentice".
- Perbaiki agar skor antarsumber sebanding. Minimal:
  (a) untuk sumber berbasis judul, satu kata generik/peran yang cocok (mis. apprentice, man, village, street) tidak cukup untuk skor tinggi; wajib ≥2 kata kunci konkret berbeda, atau 1 kata benda konkret narasi + anchor proyek;
  (b) nama diri dari narasi (mis. Mosaku, Minokichi) tidak boleh menaikkan atau menurunkan skor secara aneh;
  (c) pakai recommended_visual_sources dari preset (bila ada) sebagai bobot sumber; catat nilainya di laporan.
  Jangan mematikan sumber dan jangan membuat bobot sumber nol demi lolos tes.
- Tes wajib: shot "apprentice" tidak memilih foto militer/kapal; shot "In a village in Musashi Province…" tetap boleh memilih cetakan Hokusai (Wikimedia, judul mengandung "Musashi Province").

NARASI UJI (buat proyek folklore baru dari awal, bahasa narasi en-US, durasi pendek):
In a village in Musashi Province, west of what is now Tokyo, two woodcutters walked to the forest each morning and came back each evening. Mosaku was an old man. His apprentice, Minokichi, was eighteen.
Their way home crossed a wide river by ferry. One winter evening a snowstorm caught them on the far bank. The ferryman had gone home and left his boat on the other side, and the river was too wide and too cold to swim.
The two men took shelter in the ferryman's hut: one small room, one door, no window, and nowhere to light a fire. They pulled the door shut, lay down under their straw raincoats, and listened to the wind drive snow against the walls.
Mosaku fell asleep first. Minokichi lay awake for a long time, shivering in the dark, until the cold put him to sleep as well.
Snow on his face woke him. The door of the hut stood open. By the pale light coming off the snow, he saw a woman dressed in white bending over Mosaku and breathing on him. Her breath came out as a bright white smoke.
She turned to Minokichi and bent over him next, so low that her face nearly touched his. She was beautiful, and her eyes frightened him.
She watched him for a while before she spoke. She had meant to treat him the same way as the old man, she said, but he was young and she felt sorry for him, so she would not hurt him. If he ever told anyone what he had seen that night, even his own mother, she would know. Then she would kill him.
She went out through the open door into the storm.
