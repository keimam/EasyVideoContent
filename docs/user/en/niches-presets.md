# Niches & Custom Presets

**Niche** presets keep narration style, speaking pace (WPM), subtitle typography, and footage search context consistent across an entire project.

---

## 1. Built-in Niches & Sub-Niches

- **Built-in Niches:** Ready-to-use templates for general and themed channels (e.g., `umum.id`, `umum.en`, `automotive.muscle_car`, `automotive.jdm`). Built-in presets are read-only.
- **Sub-Niches & Topic Focus:** Under **Advanced settings** during project creation, pick a sub-niche or provide free-text focus terms to tailor both scripts and stock queries.

---

## 2. Managing Presets & Project Visual Anchors

Use **Manage Niches** in the sidebar to inspect the niche hierarchy, view keywords, and delete unused custom presets (projects currently sitting in Trash do not block preset deletion).

Inside a project (**Visual → Project Advanced Settings (Visual Anchors)**), you can fine-tune:

1. **Visual anchor terms (English, comma-separated):** Concrete on-camera subjects representing your topic (e.g., `street food, night market, wok cooking, vendor stall`). Used by AI keyword generation and candidate relevance filtering.
2. **Terms to avoid (English, comma-separated):** Stock candidate tags automatically rejected (e.g., `animal, wildlife, cartoon, plastic bottle`).
3. **Project visual summary:** A short visual description guiding scene selection.

Click **Save Settings** after edits, or **Regenerate with AI** to refresh anchors from the current topic.
