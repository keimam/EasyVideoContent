# EasyFaceless Studio User Guide (English)

Welcome to the official **EasyFaceless Studio** documentation. Select a topic below to learn how to produce faceless short and long-form videos end-to-end.

## Guide Index

1. **[Quick Start](quick-start.md)** — Complete ~10-minute workflow (Script → Voice → Visual → Render → CapCut), Short Video mode, CA1 benchmark timing estimates, and system requirements.
2. **[API & AI Provider Settings](ai-settings.md)** — Configuring LLM, TTS, AI Images, AI Video, **Backup AI providers** (*Settings → General*) with status badges & chain test, Gemini Flash models, and `402` / `404` / `429` errors.
3. **[Footage Sources, Licensing & Attribution](footage-sources.md)** — Complete table of stock & archival sources (Pixabay, Coverr, Pexels, Wikimedia, Openverse, NASA, Internet Archive, Smithsonian BYOK, Local files, External Images), quotas, **Mix best** vs **Priority**, and `credits.md`.
4. **[Niches & Custom Presets](niches-presets.md)** — Built-in niches, sub-niches, custom presets, and project **Visual Anchors**.
5. **[Visual Gallery & Shot Controls](visual-gallery.md)** — Switching candidates, **Local files**, **Partially done** status, and the 3-tab **🖼️ External Images** bridge (*Visual Context*, *Export Prompts*, *Import Images* for Google Flow & Zapi).
6. **[CapCut Desktop Export](capcut-export.md)** — Tested versions (**8.9.0** / **9.7.1**), why CapCut must be closed before export, and text size calibration.
7. **[Trash & Safe Project Deletion](trash-management.md)** — Moving projects to Trash (even while jobs run), optional CapCut draft cleanup, restore, and permanent deletion.
8. **[FAQ & Troubleshooting](faq-troubleshooting.md)** — Solutions for **Local files**, daily AI quota reset (07:00 WIB), Gemini `limit: 0`, Coverr 403 / Openverse timeouts / **Slow** profile, `Visual finished: X of Y shots`, Google Drive, and SmartScreen.
